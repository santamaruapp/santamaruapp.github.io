Unit Master

A trivia quiz about units of measurement from around the world.

About

Unit Master is a family-friendly trivia quiz about units of measurement — metric, imperial, Japanese traditional (shaku, sun, ken, ri), nautical & aviation, and scientific units. 13 categories, each with an "Elementary", "Advanced", or "Conversion Only" quiz mode, plus a Study tab where you can browse every unit's origin and trivia without taking a quiz.

Pricing

Unit Master is a one-time paid download. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions — every category and both the Quiz and Study tabs are unlocked from the moment you install it.

Content accuracy

The trivia, unit origins, and comparisons in the app are written for fun and general interest and are not sourced from a single authoritative reference; some unit histories have multiple accepted origin stories. Please don't rely on the app for precise conversions in professional, medical, legal, or safety-critical contexts. If you spot an error, we'd appreciate a note at the address above.

このアプリについて

「たんいマスター」は、世界のさまざまな単位(メートル法・ヤードポンド法・日本の尺貫法(尺・寸・間・里)・ 海事/航空単位・科学単位など)にまつわる由来・換算・豆知識を出題する、家族向けの雑学クイズアプリです。 全13カテゴリ、各カテゴリで「小学生向け」「上級」「単位換算のみ」の3つのクイズモードから選べます。 クイズとは別に、単位を一覧・タップして豆知識つきの解説を読める「べんきょう」タブもあります。
